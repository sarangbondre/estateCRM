## What the file shows
- **89 columns** in the standard vocabulary. All property_type values are in the BRD list, and there were 0 validation errors.
- The extractor does more than the BRD assumed:
  - it **splits one ad into several records**: 737 split children, with parent_record_id and split_index;
  - it **merges repeats itself** (146 exact, 255 near) and keeps first_seen_date, last_seen_date and times_seen;
  - it flags 206 **possible repeats** (possible_repeat_of);
  - it assigns **route_to** (Supply Team, Demand Team, Business Desk, and so on).
- It's a **cumulative master** that gets regenerated. This run was "migration v3→v4", which **changed record IDs**, and a
  `migration_map` sheet maps 2,128 old IDs to new ones (kept / merged / split).
- **Area** is one range with a basis (`area_sqft_min/max` + `area_basis` = Carpet / Builtup / Saleable; blank for about 60%).
  It is not separate built-up and carpet fields. Land has its own value, unit and sq ft fields.
- `review_reason` is **free text** (128 variants, joined with ";"), mostly "side defaulted to Supply" (601) and "deal type not stated" (119).
- **228 of 1,821 records are outside Mumbai/MMR** (Pune, Goa, Gujarat and others).
- The file contains **real names and phone numbers** from public newspaper ads.
- There are four CRM working columns filled by hand today (`lead_status`, `follow_up_date`, `crm_notes`, `route_to`).

## Proposed changes (recommendation for each)

| # | Topic | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Z-1 | **Field dictionary** | Replace PRD Appendix C with the **89 extractor columns exactly** (names, types, values). This is the strict-mode upload schema (D-15). The † fields in Appendix C are dropped or renamed to match. |
| Z-2 | **Area** | Store `area_sqft_min/max` + `area_basis` as given. The CRM Engine compares like with like when both bases are known. When a basis is blank it compares with a wider tolerance and shows an "area basis unknown" flag. Manual entry asks for the basis. Land area fields are kept as they are. |
| Z-3 | **Splits** | One uploaded ad = one **source ad** (raw text, source, page) with 1..n **records** (parent_record_id, split_index). A sighting links the ad to each child. Split children are never merged with each other automatically. |
| Z-4 | **Extractor dedup** | **Trust the extractor's merges** within its own master (times_seen, first/last seen update last seen in the CRM). `possible_repeat_of` becomes an **uncertain-merge review item** in the CRM. The CRM still dedups **across sources** (newspaper vs WhatsApp vs Digi vs manual) and against records already in the CRM. |
| Z-5 | **Re-uploads and ID changes** | Uploads of the master are **idempotent upserts by record_id**; unchanged rows do nothing. If the file carries a `migration_map`, it is applied first: kept = re-key, merged = merge into the target (reversible), split = re-point to the children. CRM work (calls, stages, matches, notes) follows the new ID. A master re-upload **never deletes** CRM records missing from the file. |
| Z-6 | **review_reason** | Keep the extractor's text and **derive a reason code** for grouping (side_defaulted, deal_type_missing, side_unclear, property_type_missing, other). Ask Vinit to emit codes later (not blocking). |
| Z-7 | **Records outside MMR** | **Import and keep** them, flagged "outside launch area". They're visible in search and dashboards but **excluded from queues, matching and listings** until more cities are enabled (BRD scope: loading cities outside MMR is out of Phase 1). |
| Z-8 | **CRM working columns** | `route_to` is used as the extractor's suggestion (the CRM routes by record_scope and side anyway). `lead_status`, `follow_up_date` and `crm_notes` are **ignored on import**, because the CRM owns stages and follow-ups. A one-time **initial import** may carry `crm_notes` as a note if you want to keep past work. |
| Z-9 | **Pilot data (CR-005)** | The pilot allows only sample or anonymised data. For the pilot, intake gets an **anonymise-on-import switch** (Phase 1a only). It replaces names, phones, emails and other contacts with consistent fake values, keeping everything else. Real contacts load only after the paid-plan gate. |
| Z-10 | **Blank market on Sale** (69%) | Blank means unknown (BRD). Matching treats an unknown supply market as compatible with any demand market and shows a "market unknown" flag. |

## Impact
- **PRD:** Appendix C replaced; US-01, US-07 and US-08 gain the rules above; a new US for re-upload and migration; a §3
  note on source ads and splits; the pilot anonymise switch in §8.4. v0.5.1 → v0.6.
- **HLD:** no change to boundaries or flows. intake handles upserts, migration maps and anonymisation. records owns
  source ads, splits and the outside-MMR flag. crm-engine handles the area basis and market-unknown rules.
- **Stage 4:** the intake and records LLDs are designed on the real columns.
