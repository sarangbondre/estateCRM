## What
Edge validation (request params, query, headers and bodies; event payloads in contract tests) runs **Ajv 8 (JSON Schema
2020-12)** against the schemas in `contracts/openapi/*.yaml` and `contracts/asyncapi/events.yaml`. The build step
dereferences them into `contracts/generated/`. Zod is not used for edge validation.

## Why
- F-10 asks for "input validation from generated schemas". The OpenAPI 3.1 schemas already are JSON Schema 2020-12.
  Validating against them directly means the validator **can't drift** from the approved contract (CLAUDE.md §3.3).
  Hand-written or converted Zod schemas would be a second copy of every contract.
- Ajv is already in the toolchain (F-04 contract checks, F-05 fixture validation).
- Converting 240 operations to Zod would lose fidelity (`oneOf`, `nullable` unions, `x-vocabulary`) and adds a generator
  to maintain.

## Impact
- `libs/http` depends on `ajv` + `ajv-formats` instead of `zod`. Services write no validation schemas of their own.
  Controlled vocabulary values are checked by `@11e/vocabulary` (R-11), as before.
- Error responses are unchanged: `400 validation-failed` with `errors[] {field, code, message}` (conventions §4).
- Later stages: none. Tasks and estimates are unchanged.
