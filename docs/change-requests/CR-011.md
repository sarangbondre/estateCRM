Each item is implemented and tested. Confirm it, or tell us the rule you want.

## CRM Engine (matching)
1. A single match requires the offer's area within tolerance (otherwise a 400 sq ft offer would score 71 against a 4,000 sq ft need). Smaller offers appear only inside bundles.
2. Sibling micromarkets match at a lower score (a Marol demand also sees Chakala offers). Unknown place names never match.
3. BHK is scored, not filtered: a 4BHK seeker sees a 2BHK at a lower score.
4. Matches of Expired/Paused demands close as `demand_exited` (the contract has no dedicated reason).
5. Manual bundles over budget or over area are allowed and flagged.
6. The nightly re-score selection follows the LLD (time-sensitive demands nightly, all on Sundays), not the capacity plan's rolling seventh.

## records
7. A quick-add demand during a call stays Captured. Add-supply offers start at Contacted. Price-sheet configurations start at Enriched.
8. Demand dedup: a detail missing on both sides counts fully; missing on one side counts half.
9. Voided records answer 404. A demand with several deal types gets its labels joined with " / ".

## journeys
10. Work without an active agent goes to the least-loaded active Manager/Admin (in the pilot, the Admin).
11. Exit and Watchlist events carry reason codes, never free text (it may contain personal data).
12. A price-sheet's life curve counts from the sheet date.

## listings
13. The MahaRERA number is mandatory only in production. The pilot shows "MahaRERA registration pending" (A7).
14. Publication levels never rise automatically. Offers without a generated label stay Private.
15. Saving publication settings with no change emits no audit event (LLD; the contract says "always").

## intake
16. `.xls` (legacy binary) is rejected with a request for `.xlsx`/`.csv`.
17. The AI fills blank fields only, in mapping mode, at confidence ≥ 0.70; below that the row goes to review.

## insight
18. Data operators get only upload-quality chat plans. "Active" offers = Upcoming…In process.
19. A chat question with a BHK is treated as residential (a lease is shown as "For Rent").
